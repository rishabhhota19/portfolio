## Problem
Every new course launch needed SD/SL pages, brochures and seven types of emailers. Two teams of four people (eight in total) spent 7–10 days producing them.

## What Rishabh built
- An N8N-based agentic system that drafts all collateral automatically and cuts turnaround to **one day, including reviews and approvals**.
- A fine-tuned GPT model with deep course knowledge drafts accurate, relevant marketing content.
- **Architecture:** one Head Agent coordinates two sub-agents:
  - a **Researcher**, which gathers and validates course information through SerpAPI;
  - an **Analyst**, which checks content structure and data accuracy and extracts the table of contents.
- Integrated with Google Docs for automatic document creation and real-time updates throughout the workflow.

## Outcome
- Launch collateral time fell from 7–10 days to 1 day.
- The team won Simplilearn's **Best Team Award** (Q3).
