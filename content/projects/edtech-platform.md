- An ed-tech platform where learners explore courses and join cohorts.
- Automated:
  - cohort and instructor mapping;
  - communication channels, with an agent that posts to WhatsApp;
  - payroll.
- **Stack:** Vercel (frontend and middleware), Sentry (error tracking), Umami (web analytics), Supabase (database), Razorpay (payments), plus in-house revenue analytics.
- **Admin dashboard:** all analytics, cohort creation, instructor pool, payroll and live error tracking.
