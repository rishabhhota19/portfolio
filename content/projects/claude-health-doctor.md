- Built a Doctor Skill for Claude. It contains:
  - medical resources and links the agent can visit for any query;
  - guidance on how a doctor should behave and reason;
  - a strict workflow for investigating evidence against current information.
- Organized personal medical documents into dated folders, then used Graphify to turn them into a knowledge-graph "second brain" for Claude, with documents processed into nodes and communities.
- Result: a private, queryable health assistant grounded in his own records.
