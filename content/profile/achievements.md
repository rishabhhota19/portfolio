- **QFPViT, from zero quantum** — He started with no quantum-computing background and one goal: a better attention mechanism using quantum algorithms. The result is [QFPViT](/publications/qfpvit), a fidelity-phase quantum self-attention operator for Vision Transformers, evaluated on the MedMNIST medical-imaging benchmarks and communicated to the journal Quantum. A second paper, a matched-budget dequantization study, then asked the harder question: do quantum-inspired feature maps help at all when compute and parameters are held equal? Communicated to [Quantum Machine Intelligence](/publications/quantum-feature-maps) (Springer).
- **Best Team Award, Simplilearn (Q3)** — Launching a course used to take two teams of four between seven and ten days: landing pages, brochures and seven kinds of emailer. He built an N8N-based agentic system — a head agent coordinating a researcher that gathers and validates course facts and an analyst that checks structure and data — with Google Docs created and updated automatically, and the turnaround fell to one day, reviews and approvals included.
- **LCSNet, published in Elsevier's Intelligence-Based Medicine** — [A dataset](/publications/lcsnet) of smartphone photos of teeth, shot at varying resolution and lighting, and a new lightweight segmentation architecture for dental caries that reached an F1-score of 71.7% and IoU of 56.8% with far fewer parameters and FLOPs than heavier baselines; results validated by expert dentists. Published 9 October 2025 (Impact Factor 2.3).
- **A sales-training system that scales** — [A high-throughput](/projects/sales-training-chatbot) sales-enablement chatbot for Simplilearn: a FAISS-indexed knowledge base, Gemini orchestrated through Google ADK, and a Streamlit interface for interactive training scenarios with real-time feedback, engineered for horizontal scalability.
- **Beat RBKnee** — He led clinicians and engineers at OsteosConnect to [an osteoarthritis pipeline](/projects/oa-classification) that turns a knee X-ray into a doctor's report in under two minutes: Kellgren–Lawrence grading at 98% accuracy and AUC, double-checked by segmentation, tested in a randomised controlled trial at SRM Trichy and deployed at Fortis Nagarbhavi, with more than 90% concordance with radiologists — ahead of the competitor RBKnee.
- **6th place, college-wide, BMSCE** — The final-year [COVID-19 Decision Support System](/projects/covid-decision-support), built by a team of four: an IoT-enabled home assessment that combines vitals, symptom checklists and chest X-ray or CT analysis in a two-stage pipeline, with a guided interface that asks screening questions first and an X-ray only when the first stage calls for it.
- **Published in Springer and Inderscience** — A chapter in Springer's Advanced Machine Learning for Real World Applications (the [Depression Identification Decision Support System](/publications/didss), with N. Verma) and COVID-19 work communicated to Inderscience journals.
- **2nd prize, Coca-Cola Cup, district-level football** — Competitive football is the other half of his week.
- **Poetry in the Hindi daily Amar Ujala** — Published verse, in Hindi.
