Led clinicians and engineers at OsteosConnect to an osteoarthritis pipeline that grades a knee X-ray at 98% accuracy and AUC, double-checks it with segmentation and reports to the doctor in under two minutes; tested in a randomised controlled trial and deployed at Fortis Hospital, Bengaluru. LCSNet, his lightweight caries segmentation network, is published in Elsevier's Intelligence-Based Medicine.
