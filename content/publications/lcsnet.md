- Built a dataset of smartphone photos of teeth with varying resolution and lighting.
- Proposed **LCSNet**, a new lightweight segmentation architecture for dental caries. It reached an **F1-score of 71.7% and IoU of 56.8%** with significantly fewer parameters and FLOPs than heavier baselines.
- Results were validated by expert dentists.
- Published October 9, 2025.
